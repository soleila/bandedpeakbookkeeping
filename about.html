About The Bookkeeper

     Based in Calgary, Alberta, Banded Peak Bookkeeping is a solo bookkeeping business focused on providing small Canadian companies with accurate, timely, and reliable financial services.

The owner is a Canadian-born Alberta resident whose knowledge and experience stems from studying and working in the IT and Financial sectors.

The name comes from the Banded Peak summit in Kananaskis, Alberta. Although an avid hiker, Leïla has yet to do the 35km Mount Glasgow to Banded Peak traverse, but once she does, she'll be updating this section immediately.

Fluent in English and French, Leïla enjoys reading, hiking, mountain biking, traveling, and playing sports during her spare time.

If you're looking for affordable, flexible, Canadian-owned bookkeeping services, email Leïla at info@bandedpeakbookkeeping.com

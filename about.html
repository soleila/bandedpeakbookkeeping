ABOUT

     We are a bookkeeping firm focused on providing small Canadian companies with accurate, timely, and reliable financial services.

Owner and founder, Leïla is a Canadian-born Alberta resident whose knowledge and experience stems from studying and working in the IT and Financial sectors.

The name comes from the Banded Peak summit in Kananaskis, Alberta. The name is a symbolism of enjoying the journey while striving to reach the highest peaks.

We offer our services in English and French.

If you're looking for affordable, flexible, Canadian-owned bookkeeping services, email Leïla at info@bandedpeakbookkeeping.com
